The goal of this policy is simple: users should be able to tell whether they are getting the official software, and nobody should be able to sell or promote something else under the project's name.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name to **refer to the project truthfully**, for example in articles, tutorials, reviews, talks, and comparisons.
- Say that your software or service is **"compatible with"**, **"works with"**, or **"based on"** the project, as long as you do not suggest endorsement and your own name is clearly more prominent.
- **Redistribute unmodified official releases** under the original name, including in Linux distribution packages. Packaging changes that do not alter functionality (paths, init integration, dependency unbundling) count as unmodified.
- Use the name and logo for **community activities** that do not charge for participation (user groups, meetups, non-commercial merchandise for your own use).

## You must ask for written permission to

- Use the Marks for a **modified version** of the software. Forks are welcome under the license, but they must use a different name and logo and remove ours from the UI, installers, and documentation. A statement such as "based on Unpanel" is fine.
- Use the Marks in the name of a **product, service, company, or domain name**, for example "Unpanel Hosting" or `unpanel-hosting.example`.
- Offer **commercial services** (hosting, support, training) in a way that uses the Marks beyond truthful reference.
- Sell **merchandise** bearing the Marks.

## Always

- Do not use the Marks in a way that is misleading, suggests that CodeNav Ltd endorses you, or damages the project's reputation.
- Do not use confusingly similar names or logos.
- Do not register the Marks, or names similar to them, as trademarks, domains, or social media accounts.
